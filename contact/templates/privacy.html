Who Is Responsible

Avonmoor Ltd, company number 04655485, is the data controller responsible for personal information submitted through this website. Its registered office is Victoria Cottage, Church Street, South Brent, Devon, TQ10 9AB. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to hello@avonmoor.co.uk.

Contact Enquiries

When you use the contact form, we collect the details you enter, which may include your name, email address, phone number, postcode, chosen subject and message. We use them to understand and respond to your enquiry and to keep an appropriate record of the conversation.

Rainwater Calculator

Your property address and calculation inputs are held in an essential website session for up to one hour so the calculator can work. The headline calculation does not create a customer lead.

If you choose to unlock the detailed estimate, we save your name, email address, optional phone number, property and roof details, calculated figures and the time of your consent. We use these details to provide the full estimate and make a relevant follow-up about rainwater harvesting. You are not added to an automated mailing list.

Why We Can Use It

We use enquiry details to take steps you request and for our legitimate interest in responding and keeping appropriate business records. Where you give consent for the detailed calculator estimate and follow-up, you can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing Avonmoor. Withdrawal does not affect use that was lawful before it was withdrawn.

Maps and Postcode Lookup

The aerial roof tool uses Google Maps. When it loads, Google may receive map and address-related information under Google's privacy terms. If you choose the manual fallback, your postcode is sent to Postcodes.io to obtain approximate postcode-centre coordinates. The fallback does not send your name, email address or phone number to Postcodes.io.

How Long We Keep Information

Enquiry and estimate records are kept only while reasonably needed to respond, provide the requested service, maintain appropriate business records and meet legal obligations. Records are reviewed and deleted when they are no longer needed.

Who Helps Us Process It

Website hosting and email providers may process information for Avonmoor so the site can operate and enquiries can be delivered. We disclose information where legally required, but do not sell personal information.

Your Choices and Rights

You can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or object to or restrict how it is used, by emailing Avonmoor. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Essential Website Data

The site uses essential session and security data needed to operate forms and the calculator. Avonmoor does not use this calculator submission to add you to marketing tracking or an automated mailing list.
