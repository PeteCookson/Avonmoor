Assessment limitations

This report is a preliminary rainwater assessment, not a quotation or final system design. Yield is based on the horizontal roof footprint, recorded runoff and system-efficiency factors, and the stated rainfall dataset. The storage figure uses an 18-day balance of the lower annual yield or recorded non-potable demand, rounded to a practical capacity. The representative water balance distributes monthly yield and demand evenly across each month. Final sizing, back-up supply, water quality, components and installation must be confirmed for the actual site and checked against BS EN 16941-1:2024 and applicable water regulations.
